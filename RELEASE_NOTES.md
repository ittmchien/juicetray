## What's new in v0.2.2

### ✨ New
- **Add device…** — plug in a wireless receiver, press **Try** to read its battery once, then **Add** it. Works for many ATK, VXE, VGN, AULA and Weikav models that are not verified yet.
- **Send report** — if a device can't be read, send its details through a short Google Form (no account needed) or open a GitHub issue. Personal info (user folder, PC name, serials) is removed.
- **New-device popup** — shown when you plug in a supported or wireless device, once per device.
- 📋 [Supported and requested devices](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/e/2PACX-1vRTKUOHRmpLeXhjxL93T5HwuYsJKnzMHSUhN2AWslNCB938EpQH2YkMeNubTcpvdxpHC2o7Dw4OHfS7/pubhtml)

### 🎨 Improved
- Modern Windows 10/11 look for all dialogs.
- Options moved under **Settings**; offline devices now say *disconnected*.
- Xbox and other Bluetooth LE controllers show in the Controller group.
- Checks for updates at most once a day.

### 🛠 Fixed
- Settings are never overwritten when the config file can't be read.
- Update now works when Juicetray was installed with winget.

Download `juicetray-windows-x86_64.exe` below, or use **Update now** in the app.
